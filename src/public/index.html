TikTok Subtitle Format Comparison: SRT vs. VTT vs. TXT

Understanding the difference between subtitle formats ensures your captions align perfectly with your editing software or workflow.

Format Extension Timestamps Best Use Case Software Compatibility
SubRip Subtitle .SRT Millisecond precision (00:00:01,500) Short-form video editing, YouTube Shorts, Reels CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro
WebVTT .VTT Web standard (00:00:01.500) HTML5 web video, browser players, eLearning Chrome, Safari, Video.js, HTML5 <track>, Vimeo
Plain Text .TXT No timestamps (speech only) Reading, summarization, repurposing, AI prompts ChatGPT, Claude, Notion, Google Docs, Apple Notes
